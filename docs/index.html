Why Instalite?

Instagram Reels are designed to be addictive — infinite scroll, autoplay, algorithm-driven content engineered to keep you watching. Instalite removes that one tab so you can use Instagram intentionally, not compulsively. Keep the parts that connect you to people. Lose the parts that waste your time.

FAQ

Does this work on iPhone?

Yes. Instalite is built for iOS 16 and above. It runs on any iPhone supported by iOS 16+.

Do I need a paid Apple Developer account?

No. A free Apple ID is enough to build and run Instalite on your own device via Xcode.

Will my login be saved?

Yes. Your Instagram session is stored locally on your device using cookies — just like Safari. You won't need to log in every time.

Can I still post stories and photos?

Yes. Camera and photo library access are fully supported. You can post stories, photos and videos as normal.

Is this open source?

Yes. Instalite is fully open source under the MIT license. You can inspect, modify and distribute the code freely.
